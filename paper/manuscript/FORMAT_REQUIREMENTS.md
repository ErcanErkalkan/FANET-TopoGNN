# Manuscript presentation settings (Wireless Networks)

The **scientific research content** is intentionally not framed as belonging to a journal. Only the presentation layer of the manuscript targets the chosen publication venue, *Wireless Networks* (Springer Nature).

Publisher instructions consulted 2026-11-21: https://link.springer.com/journal/11276/submission-guidelines

- Manuscript: A4, 10 pt, double line spacing; 25.4 mm top/bottom and 31.75 mm left/right margins.
- Font: XeLaTeX chooses Times New Roman when installed, otherwise Tinos; the default pdflatex build uses Times-compatible NewTX. Neither substitute is proof of **exact** Times New Roman compliance.
- References: numeric citations in square brackets, number-ordered bibliography.
- Abstract: 150-250 words. Keywords: 4-6.
- 20-page body limit excludes bibliography and figures, and needs explicit verification in the final formatted PDF. Tables are not explicitly excluded from this limit.
- Include editable `.tex` and `.bib` with submission; data availability and declarations require author verification.
- The default build uses `pdflatex` for Springer Nature automated-submission compatibility. Source also supports manual XeLaTeX compilation for the exact installed Times New Roman font. Springer recommends their `sn-jnl` authoring template; the present standalone draft applies the journal-level formatting rules directly, and is **not** represented as the official sn-jnl template. Validate with the final submission portal before sending.
